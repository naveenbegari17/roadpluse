About RoadPulse — Roads That Speak

Empowering citizens to transform everyday road hazards into actionable, prioritized intelligence without expensive proprietary infrastructure.

1. The Mission & College Project Vision

RoadPulse was engineered as a zero-cost demonstration platform for a first-year B.Tech engineering demonstration. In fast-growing urban hubs like Hyderabad and across global developing corridors, potholes, unlit highway stretches, and flash waterlogging persist unnoticed until an accident occurs.

RoadPulse bridges the communication gap by providing an open, community-accessible platform where complaints are weighted mathematically to highlight which physical locations demand urgent triage.

2. Road Risk Score Mathematical Formula

Every submitted hazard is processed by services/risk_engine.py using an objective composite formula:

ROAD RISK SCORE = (Severity Score × 0.50) + (Repeat Score × 0.30) + (Recency Score × 0.20)

Severity (50%)

Low = 20, Medium = 45, High = 75, Critical = 100. Anchors the baseline threat level.

Repeat Complaints (30%)

Uses spatial Haversine clustering (~500m radius). Capped between 0 and 100 to prevent score manipulation.

Recency Decay (20%)

<24 hours: 100, 1-3 days: 80, 3-7 days: 50, decaying gracefully over 30 days to reflect freshness.

⚠️ Critical Transparency & Academic Limitation Disclaimer

1. Not an Accident Prediction Model: The Road Risk Score is an experimental community prioritization tool, not a scientifically certified traffic accident predictor.
2. Unverified Reports: Citizen submissions are unverified and represent open crowdsourced inputs.
3. No Official Government Affiliation: RoadPulse is an educational prototype and does not have an active data dispatch integration with municipal road authorities.
