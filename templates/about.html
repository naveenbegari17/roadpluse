About RoadPulse — Roads That Speak

Empowering communities to convert everyday road hazards into actionable, prioritized intelligence without expensive proprietary infrastructure.

1. The Mission & College Project Vision

RoadPulse was engineered as a zero-cost demonstration platform for a first-year B.Tech engineering project. In rapidly expanding urban centers like Hyderabad, critical hazards—from sunken craters to dark unlit corridors—often languish unaddressed due to fragmented reporting.

RoadPulse delivers an open civic framework where crowdsourced complaints are dynamically scored and prioritized, giving community advocates clear data on which dangerous hotspots need remediation first.

2. Road Risk Score Mathematical Formula

Every submitted hazard is computed inside services/risk_engine.py using an objective composite formula:

ROAD RISK SCORE = (Severity × 0.50) + (Repeat Score × 0.30) + (Recency Score × 0.20)

Severity (50%)

Low = 20, Medium = 45, High = 75, Critical = 100. Anchors the initial baseline threat.

Repeat Complaints (30%)

Spatial Haversine clustering (~500m radius). Capped between 0 and 100 to prevent runaway abuse.

Recency Decay (20%)

<24 hours: 100, 1-3 days: 80, 3-7 days: 50, decaying smoothly over 30 days.

⚠️ Critical Transparency & Academic Limitation Disclaimer

1. Not an Accident Prediction Model: The Road Risk Score is an experimental prioritization metric for community triage and not an accident forecast.
2. Unverified Reports: Citizen submissions are unverified crowdsourced observations.
3. No Official Government Affiliation: RoadPulse is an educational prototype and is not officially linked to municipal dispatch systems.
